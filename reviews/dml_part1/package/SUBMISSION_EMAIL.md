coverings of even-dimensional binary hypercubes. It improves the published

## Optional sentence — insert only after an affirmative policy reply

Place immediately before "Thank you for considering the manuscript.", filling in
the real date and name:

> On [DATE], [NAME] confirmed that the disclosed use of generative language
> models is acceptable under the journal's policy.

Insert this **only** after an unequivocally affirmative reply, and only
stating what the answer was. A reply that merely says "you may submit and it
will be considered" does **not** justify claiming the use was confirmed
acceptable — in that case omit the sentence entirely. Never include a sentence
that records an enquiry was made without saying how it was resolved.

## Why it is this short

The AI disclosure, the funding statement and the conflict-of-interest statement
all live in the manuscript's Acknowledgment, which is where DML's ethics rules
require them. Repeating them here would make the tooling, rather than the
theorem, the last substantive thing the editor reads before opening the PDF.
