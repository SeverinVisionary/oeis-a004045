THE QUANTITY
K(8,1,2): minimum size of a SET C of binary words of length 8 with
|C intersect B_1(v)| >= 2 for every v in F_2^8, where B_1 has 9 words.
OEIS A004045(8). Published record 59 <= K(8,1,2) <= 64 (Krotov-Potapov
arXiv:1902.00023 Thm 6(a) 2020; Ostergard tabu search 1995).
Equivalently the 2-tuple/double domination number gamma_{x2}(Q_8) with CLOSED
neighbourhoods, and equivalently 256 - L_7(Q_8) where L_7 is the maximum
7-limited packing number. A stock 0/1 IP over 256 word-variables, in HiGHS and
in SCIP, refutes M = 57, 58, 59, 60 in seconds to minutes. That would give
K >= 61. I am trying to find that somebody already knew this.
